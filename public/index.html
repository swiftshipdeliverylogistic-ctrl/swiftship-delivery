About SwiftShip Delivery

SwiftShip Delivery is a modern delivery platform designed to make sending and receiving packages simple, transparent, and reliable.

Our goal is to give individuals and businesses a straightforward way to book deliveries, track shipments, and manage their logistics — all in one place.

This platform is actively being developed. Features are being added progressively, and integrations with maps, payments, and notification services are planned for future releases.

Note: Contact details shown on this site are placeholders and should be replaced with real information before public use.

Privacy Policy

We collect only the information needed to provide our delivery services — such as your name, contact details, and delivery addresses. We do not sell your data to third parties. Full policy available on request.

Terms of Service

By using SwiftShip Delivery, you agree to provide accurate delivery information and to follow all applicable laws when sending packages. Service availability and pricing may change.

Refund & Cancellation Policy

Deliveries may be cancelled before pickup. Once a package is collected, cancellation and refund rules depend on the delivery status. Contact support for specific requests.
